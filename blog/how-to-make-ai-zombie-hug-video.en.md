The viral AI zombie hug (also called Zombie Love Story) is a short vertical clip: someone you love has turned, you cannot shoot, and the leap becomes a warm memory hug. You can make one on [aizombie.app](https://aizombie.app) without creating an account.

## What you need

- Two photos you own or have permission to use (one adult per photo for couple stories; pet + you for cat/dog templates)
- About one minute to upload and pay
- A phone or laptop browser

Do not upload children’s photos or faces you do not have rights to use.

## Steps

1. Open [aizombie.app](https://aizombie.app) (or pick a template under `/effects`).
2. Upload **Photo 1** (holds the gun / survivor) and **Photo 2** (turns into the zombie, or your pet).
3. Optional: add one line for the memory place (snowy slope, summer meadow, etc.).
4. Choose 9:16 for TikTok / Reels / Shorts, and 720p or 480p.
5. Press **Create**. If you need credits, Stripe Checkout opens — guests can pay.
6. Wait for Seedance 2.5 to finish, then download the MP4.
